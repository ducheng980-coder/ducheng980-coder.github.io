How to use the Virtual Try-On

  1. Upload a photo of yourself (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 MB).
  2. Upload a garment product image — a transparent PNG works best; for a white-background product shot, use “Crop garment” to trim the extra background away.
  3. Drag the garment into place; resize it with two-finger pinch or the Size slider, and fine-tune with the rotation and opacity sliders.
  4. Select “Export PNG” to save the composite at your photo’s full working resolution.

Formula and method

The composite is a plain 2D affine placement: the garment (or the part of it inside your crop rectangle) is scaled to the chosen width, rotated around its centre by the chosen angle, drawn over your photo at the chosen position with the chosen opacity, and the canvas is exported as a PNG. No body detection, no warping, no AI — what you align is exactly what you get.

Example

A shopper saves a jacket’s product photo, crops away the white margins, places it over a full-body photo at about 55% of the photo width, rotates it 3° to match a slight lean, and exports the PNG to share for a second opinion. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Is this AI virtual try-on?

No. This first version is manual compositing: you place the garment image yourself with drag, scale, rotation and opacity controls. It gives a quick impression of colour and proportion, not a physically accurate fit.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Both images are processed entirely in your browser with the Canvas API. They are never sent to a server, never stored, and are released from memory when you clear them or close the page. As with every tool on this site, the only thing recorded is an anonymous “tool used” count — never image data.

Which garment images work best?

A front-facing product photo on a transparent background (PNG) blends best. For the usual white-background marketplace photo, crop tightly around the garment first — leftover white margins are the main thing that makes a composite look pasted-on. Lowering the opacity slightly can also help it sit more naturally.

What resolution is the export?

The export matches your photo’s working size exactly. Photos larger than 4096 px on the long edge are scaled down proportionally on load (the page tells you when this happens) so editing stays smooth on phones.

Why does the garment look flat?

A flat product image has no folds, shading or body occlusion, and a 2D overlay cannot invent them. Treat the result as a styling preview — colour, length and rough proportion — not as a fitting guarantee.
