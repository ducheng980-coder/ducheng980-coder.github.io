PDF Page Picker & Combiner

Need page 2 from one report, pages 5–8 from another, and the cover from a third — in one file, in your order? Add several PDFs, tap the page thumbnails you want (or type ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-10), drag the picked pages into order, and download a single combined PDF. Everything runs locally in your browser: your files are never uploaded to a server, and nothing is stored after you leave the page.

How to use the PDF Page Picker & Combiner

  1. Choose or drop one or more PDF files.
  2. Tap page thumbnails to pick or unpick them, or type a page range (for example 1-3, 5, 8-10) and apply it.
  3. Drag the picked pages into order — on a phone use the up / down buttons instead.
  4. Check the compatibility note, set an output file name, and select “Generate PDF” to download the combined file.

Formula and method

Page thumbnails are rendered locally with pdf.js; the output PDF is assembled with pdf-lib, which copies each picked page — keeping its original size and orientation — into a new document in your order. Copied pages keep their content, but document-level structures do not carry over: digital signatures, interactive form fields, bookmarks, annotations, attachments and document metadata are dropped. Encrypted (password-protected) PDFs cannot be processed — the tool never tries to bypass a password.

Example

From three sample files (A: 3 pages, B: 4 pages, C: 2 pages), picking A-1, B-4, C-1, A-3 and B-2 produces a 5-page PDF in exactly that order, with each page keeping its original size and orientation. To try it, add your own PDFs above — click to choose files, or drop them in — then pick pages and select “Generate PDF”.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read into your browser’s memory and processed there with pdf.js and pdf-lib. You can verify it: open this page, switch off your network, and the tool still works — and the browser’s Network tab shows no upload request while you work.

Can I pick pages from several PDFs at once?

Yes. Add as many PDFs as you like; every picked page goes into one shared output list, and you can reorder pages across files freely before generating the combined PDF.

What happens to digital signatures, forms and bookmarks?

They are not carried over. The output contains the picked pages’ content only — signatures become invalid, form fields are flattened away, and bookmarks, comments, attachments and metadata are dropped. The page warns you about this before you generate the file.

Why won’t it open my password-protected PDF?

Encrypted PDFs need their password to be read, and this tool deliberately does not ask for or bypass passwords. Remove the password in your own PDF reader first (if you are allowed to), then use the unlocked copy here.

Is there a file-size limit?

No hard limit, but everything happens in your browser’s memory: very large files or hundreds of pages slow down thumbnail rendering and may use a lot of RAM. Work with a few files at a time for the smoothest experience.
