X Post Analyzer

Curious what a viral post is actually worth? Paste the link of any public X (Twitter) post and this tool pulls its live public counters — views, likes, reposts, replies, quotes and bookmarks — through the free FxTwitter API, then works out the numbers people actually argue about: total engagements, engagement rate, bookmark rate, average views per hour, a reference heat tier, and a rough estimate of the creator revenue share the post could have earned. It is the quickest way to sanity-check claims like “one post, half a million views” before believing the income screenshots that come with them.

Data comes from the public FxTwitter API (api.fxtwitter.com), which mirrors the post’s public counters on X. Figures are the values at the moment you run the analysis and keep changing as the post spreads.

How to use the X Post Analyzer

  1. Paste a full X post link (x.com or twitter.com, share links with extra parameters are fine).
  2. Select “Analyse post” and read the counters and rates.
  3. Use “Try the example post” to see a worked analysis of a real viral post.

Formula and method

Total engagements = likes + reposts + replies + quotes + bookmarks. Engagement rate = total engagements ÷ views; bookmark rate = bookmarks ÷ views; like rate = likes ÷ views. Average views per hour = views ÷ hours since the post was published. Heat tiers are a reference scale by views: under 10k everyday, 10k–100k popular, 100k–500k a hit, over 500k phenomenal. The payout estimate multiplies views by US$8–12 per million views and converts at ¥7.1 per US dollar — it assumes every view were an eligible view, which real payouts never are (see the FAQ).

Example

The example post — a long-form post by @404jsh — passed 200,000 views with over 2,100 bookmarks, an engagement rate of about 1.5%, and an estimated payout of roughly $1.6–$2.5 (about ¥12–¥17) under the stated assumptions. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

Your browser asks the free public FxTwitter API (api.fxtwitter.com) for the post. It mirrors the public counters X itself displays, so the numbers are the post’s real public figures at the moment you run the analysis — they keep changing as the post spreads.

How accurate is the payout estimate?

Treat it as an optimistic ceiling, not a prediction. X pays creators only for engagement from Premium (paying) users, and the account must first qualify — a Premium subscription, at least 500 followers and 5 million impressions in the last 3 months, with a minimum payout threshold. Most views are not Premium engagement, so real payouts are usually far below the estimate, and unqualified accounts earn nothing at all.

Which links work?

Any public post link from x.com or twitter.com, including share links with tracking parameters and /photo/1 style suffixes. Deleted posts and posts from protected (private) accounts cannot be fetched.

Is the link I paste stored?

Not by this site. Your browser sends the post reference directly to api.fxtwitter.com over HTTPS and the answer comes straight back to your browser; this page keeps no copy. As with every tool here, only an anonymous “tool used” count is recorded for our own statistics.
