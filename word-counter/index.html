How to use the Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box.
  2. Read the counts — they update instantly as you edit.
  3. Use “Load example” to see how it works, or “Clear” to start over.

Formula and method

Words are counted as sequences of letters or numbers separated by spaces or punctuation. Reading time assumes an average adult reading speed of 200 words per minute; speaking time assumes 130 words per minute.

Frequently asked questions

Does the counter store my text?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved on a server.

How is reading time calculated?

We divide the word count by 200 words per minute, a commonly used average for adult silent reading. Your own pace may be faster or slower.

Do numbers count as words?

Yes — a run of digits such as “2026” counts as one word, matching how most editors count.
