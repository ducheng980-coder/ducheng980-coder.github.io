How to use the Case Converter

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. Choose a case style from the list.
  3. Copy the result with the copy button.

Formula and method

Title Case capitalises the first letter of each word. camelCase joins words with no spaces and capitalises every word except the first; PascalCase capitalises all of them. snake_case and kebab-case join lowercase words with _ or -.

Example

“the quick brown fox” becomes “The Quick Brown Fox” in Title Case and “theQuickBrownFox” in camelCase. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded?

No. Conversion runs in your browser only.

Which case do programmers use?

camelCase for variables in JavaScript, PascalCase for class names, snake_case in Python, and kebab-case for URLs and CSS.
