Unit Converter

One converter for the units everyday life mixes up: metres and miles, kilograms and pounds, Celsius and Fahrenheit, gigabytes and gibibytes, and more. Pick a category, choose units, and get the result with the exact conversion factor used.

How to use the Unit Converter

  1. Pick a category (length, weight, temperature, …).
  2. Choose the from- and to-units — the lists update with the category.
  3. Enter a value and calculate.

Formula and method

Each unit is defined by an exact factor to its category base unit (for example 1 inch = 0.0254 m exactly). Result = value × factor(from) ÷ factor(to). Temperature uses the standard Celsius/Fahrenheit/Kelvin formulas. Data uses decimal KB–TB (powers of 1000) and binary KiB–GiB (powers of 1024), labelled separately.

Example

10 kilometres is 6.2137 miles; 100 °F is 37.78 °C. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Why are KB and KiB both listed?

Storage makers use decimal (1 KB = 1,000 bytes) while operating systems often report binary (1 KiB = 1,024 bytes). Both are provided so you can match the figure you are checking.

How precise are the factors?

They are the standard international definitions (NIST), so conversions are exact to the digits shown.
