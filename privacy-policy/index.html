Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026. This policy explains what data MFXGJ does and does not collect.

Your calculator inputs

All calculations run locally in your web browser. The text and numbers you type into a tool are never transmitted to us, never stored on a server, and are gone when you close the tab. The “recently used” list on the home page is stored only in your own browser’s local storage, which you can clear at any time in your browser settings.

Analytics

If privacy-respecting aggregate analytics (such as Google Analytics 4) are enabled on the production site, they measure page views and general usage (pages visited, approximate country, device type) — never the contents of your calculations. Analytics providers may set cookies as described below.

Advertising and cookies

Once approved, this site may display advertising through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message managed through a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, as Google requires. Declining consent never blocks access to the calculators.

Server logs

Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) processes standard technical logs (IP address, user agent, timestamps) to deliver pages and prevent abuse. We do not build profiles from these logs or sell any data.

Your choices

  • Clear local storage and cookies in your browser at any time.
  • Use the site with JavaScript-based analytics blocked — the calculators still work offline in the page once loaded.
  • Contact us with any privacy question via the Contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes materially (for example, when advertising or analytics are switched on), we will update this page and the “last updated” date before the change takes effect.
