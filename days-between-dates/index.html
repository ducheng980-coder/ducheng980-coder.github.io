Days Between Dates

Sometimes you only need one number: how many days between two dates. Enter a start and an end date to get the day count, with weeks, approximate months and weekday counts as supporting detail.

Advertisement

How to use the Days Between Dates

  1. Enter the start date.
  2. Enter the end date.
  3. Select “Calculate” — the headline is the day count.

Formula and method

Days = end date − start date, measured at midnight in your local timezone, so daylight-saving transitions do not skew the count.

Frequently asked questions

Does the order of dates matter?

No. If the end date is earlier, the tool swaps them and reports the span; the total-days figure keeps the sign of end minus start.

What is the difference from the date difference calculator?

This page leads with the simple day count; the date difference calculator leads with the years/months/days breakdown. The underlying math is identical.
