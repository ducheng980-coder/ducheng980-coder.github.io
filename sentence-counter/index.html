How to use the Sentence Counter

  1. Paste your text into the box.
  2. See the sentence count plus total words to judge average sentence length.
  3. Shorten or split long sentences and re-check.

Formula and method

A sentence ends at a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark (including runs like “…” or “?!”) followed by more text or the end of the text. Abbreviations such as “e.g.” may be counted imperfectly — treat the result as a close estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good average sentence length?

Plain-English guidance often suggests averaging 15–20 words per sentence for general readers, mixing short and longer sentences for rhythm.

Are abbreviations handled?

Mostly, but a period inside an abbreviation can look like a sentence end to a simple counter. Proofread borderline cases.
