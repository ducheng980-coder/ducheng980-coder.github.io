Date Calculator

Need the date 90 days from now, or 6 months before a deadline? Pick a start date, choose add or subtract, and enter an amount of days, weeks, months or years. The result shows the new date, its weekday, and how many days you actually moved.

How to use the Date Calculator

  1. Pick a start date.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract and a unit (days, weeks, months, years).
  3. Enter the amount and select “Calculate”.

Formula and method

Days and weeks are exact. Months and years use calendar arithmetic and clamp to the end of shorter months (for example, 31 January + 1 month = the last day of February).

Example

1 March 2026 + 90 days = 30 May 2026. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Jan 31 + 1 month not Mar 3?

Calendar month addition keeps the same day-of-month when possible and clamps to the last valid day otherwise — February has no 31st.

Does it count business days?

No — this calculator moves calendar days. The date difference calculator reports weekdays between two dates.
