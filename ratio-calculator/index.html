Ratio Calculator

Enter two numbers to simplify their ratio (for recipes, screen sizes, maps and mixtures), see each side’s percentage share, and optionally split a total amount in that ratio.

How to use the Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter the two sides of the ratio (a and b).
  2. Optionally enter a total to split.
  3. Select “Calculate”.

Formula and method

Whole-number ratios are simplified by dividing both sides by their greatest common divisor. Shares are a/(a+b) and b/(a+b); a total is split in the same proportions.

Example

The ratio 12:8 simplifies to 3:2, with shares of 60% and 40%. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How do I scale a ratio up?

Multiply both sides by the same number — 3:2 scaled by 10 is 30:20. Use the total field to split an amount instead.

What aspect ratio is 1920×1080?

1920:1080 simplifies to 16:9 — enter a=1920, b=1080 to check any resolution.
