BMI Calculator

Body mass index (BMI) is a screening number that compares weight to height. Enter yours — metric or imperial — to see your BMI, its standard adult category, and the healthy weight range for your height.

Health figures on this page are general information only and are not medical advice.

How to use the BMI Calculator

  1. Choose metric (kg, cm) or imperial (lb, in).
  2. Enter your weight and height.
  3. Select “Calculate”.

Formula and method

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Adult categories (WHO): under 18.5 underweight, 18.5–24.9 healthy, 25–29.9 overweight, 30+ obesity. BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not designed for children, athletes or pregnancy.

Example

At 70 kg and 175 cm, BMI is 22.9 — inside the healthy range. Use the “Load example” button above to try it with the sample inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. It is a rough screening measure. Body composition, age, sex and ethnicity all matter — discuss your result with a health professional.

Does BMI work for children?

Children use age- and sex-specific percentiles instead of the adult cut-offs shown here.
